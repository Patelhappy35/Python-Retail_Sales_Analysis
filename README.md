# Retail Sales Analysis and Business Insights using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes an Indian retail sales dataset using Python to understand business performance, sales trends, regional performance, product categories, customer types, and city-wise revenue.

The project uses **Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn** for data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), business analysis, and data visualization.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall retail sales performance
* Identify high-performing product categories
* Compare revenue across different regions
* Analyze monthly revenue trends
* Compare revenue based on customer type
* Identify top-performing cities
* Analyze the relationship between discounts and revenue
* Generate useful business recommendations

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

## 🔍 Data Cleaning & EDA

The dataset was analyzed for:

* Missing values
* Data types
* Duplicate records
* Summary statistics
* Date conversion and month extraction

The `Order_date` column was converted into datetime format, and a `Month` column was created for monthly sales analysis.

## 📊 Business Analysis

The project includes:

* **Region-wise Revenue Analysis**
* **Product Category-wise Sales Performance**
* **Customer Type Revenue Comparison**
* **Monthly Sales Trend**
* **Top 5 Cities by Revenue**

## 📈 Data Visualizations

The project contains different visualizations to understand business performance:

* Revenue by Product Category — Bar Chart
* Monthly Revenue Trend — Line Chart
* Region Revenue Distribution — Pie Chart
* Revenue Trend by Discount — Line Chart
* Top 5 Cities by Revenue — Bar Chart

## 💡 Key Insights

* Electronics shows strong revenue performance among product categories.
* West and South regions contribute a significant share of revenue.
* Mumbai and Surat are among the major revenue-generating cities.
* Monthly revenue fluctuates across the year and is not completely consistent.
* The analysis indicates a negative relationship between discount percentage and revenue in this dataset.

## 💼 Business Recommendations

1. Focus on high-performing categories such as Electronics and Home & Kitchen.
2. Improve performance in low-performing cities while maintaining strong-performing locations.
3. Optimize pricing and discount strategies to support revenue generation.
4. Focus on improving consistency during low-performing months.
